07 / YOUR QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is the starting price?+

Prices start from AED 689,000 (approximately USD 187,611), subject to current availability. Request a unit-specific quotation including registration and administration fees.

When is handover expected?+

The factbook indicates December 2029, within Q4 2029. This is an anticipated completion date, subject to the developer and contractual terms.

Can I buy remotely with only a passport?+

Remote purchase can be arranged. A valid passport is needed to begin; further identity, address, compliance or source-of-funds documents may be required. A passport alone does not guarantee completion.

Do I need salary slips for the payment plan?+

This is a developer instalment plan, not a bank mortgage. Salary slips are not necessarily required in the same way as mortgage underwriting, but the developer and compliance teams may request supporting documents.

Is investing in Dubai completely tax free?+

Qualifying personal property investment income and gains are generally outside UAE corporate tax. This does not remove registration charges, service fees or potential taxes in your country of tax residence.

Does this purchase qualify for a Golden Visa?+

Not automatically. The Golden Visa generally requires qualifying property investment of AED 2 million or more and other conditions. Investor residency follows separate eligibility and title requirements.

Is there an agency commission?+

The offer on this site includes 0% buyer agency commission. Government, registration, administrative and ongoing ownership costs are separate. Confirm the full cost sheet before booking.

How does the 1% payment plan work?+

Pay 15% on booking, 5% after six months, 5% after twelve months and 75 instalments of 1%. The monthly and milestone payments total 100% of the price. Your signed schedule controls exact dates.
